# Current Operations Context and Potential Value

## What is known from the project brief

The leadership materials describe White Center Food Bank as coordinating:
- Food distribution.
- Volunteer participation.
- Home-delivery activity.
- Community partnerships.

The materials identify CERVIS and current procedures as part of the existing operating environment. They do not establish the full set of WCFB software, partner systems, staffing arrangements, intake channels, routing tools, reporting practices, or internal controls.

**Important:** We have not independently audited WCFB operations. We must not claim that any system is deficient, that staff rely on paper, that undocumented overrides occur, or that particular partners or integrations are in place unless WCFB confirms it.

## How Civic Continuum and Maya Node could potentially help

The potential value is not replacing WCFB's food-bank management systems. It is testing whether a narrow accountability layer could make selected handoffs and follow-through easier to understand.

| Operational question | Possible Maya Node contribution to explore | Human authority retained |
|---|---|---|
| Who prepared, approved, and acknowledged a handoff? | A structured fictional record of the handoff and its next step. | WCFB decides who may approve and accept. |
| Who should review an unusual delivery or quantity discrepancy? | A clearly assigned exception record with a human-entered decision and next step. | WCFB decides food acceptance and all food-safety matters. |
| Who owns a concern and what happened afterward? | A visible owner, status, corrective step, and human-entered closeout note. | WCFB determines the response and whether the issue is resolved. |
| What should happen when a record is incomplete? | A prompt to identify missing information and the person responsible for clarifying it. | Staff decide whether and how to correct the record. |

## Potential benefits to evaluate—not promised outcomes

1. **Clearer accountability:** People can more easily see who owns a step and who has authority to decide.
2. **More legible handoffs:** A request, decision, acknowledgement, and next step can be viewed together.
3. **Exception visibility:** Unusual cases may be easier to distinguish from routine work and route to a human decision-maker.
4. **Follow-through:** Open concerns, assigned owners, and human-entered completion notes may be easier to review.
5. **Governance and learning:** If the organization chooses, recurring themes and corrective actions may be summarized for internal learning.
6. **Respect for existing tools:** A narrowly scoped layer could be evaluated alongside current systems rather than displacing them.

## Discovery questions before proposing any integration

- Which systems and tools are currently used for scheduling, volunteer coordination, inventory, intake, home delivery, reporting, and partner coordination?
- Which records are authoritative?
- Where are staff already required to enter the same information more than once?
- Which workflows already work well and should not be disturbed?
- What are the real handoff or follow-through pain points, if any?
- Who owns each decision, and what policies govern it?
- What accessibility, language, privacy, security, retention, and reporting requirements apply?
- What would constitute measurable improvement, and what would count as unacceptable extra work?

## Current prototype boundary

The current workflow lab is browser-based and offline, using fictional data and simulated interactions. It is not a live WCFB management system. There is no live integration with CERVIS or any other WCFB system, no live dispatch or routing, no driver tracking, no delivery verification, no food-safety monitoring or approval, and no claim of production-grade audit retention or compliance certification.

## Recommended posture

Listen first. Ask staff to choose one workflow. Demonstrate only with synthetic data. Compare the concept against the way the work is already done. Be prepared to document that the existing process is better and that no change is warranted.
