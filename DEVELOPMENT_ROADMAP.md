# Development Roadmap and Readiness Gates

Progress is based on evidence, not optimistic status labels.

## Stage 1 — Presentation readiness
- Complete the eight-slide leadership deck and matching presenter script.
- Include all three scenarios and the potential organizational value to evaluate.
- Remove private personal background, unverified metrics, unsupported partner/system claims, and any claim that the prototype is already operational.
- **Completion test:** a reviewer can distinguish the proposal, current prototype, and future requirements.

## Stage 2 — Technical proof
- Select one narrow workflow, initially a fictional volunteer handoff.
- Run the offline lab from a clean browser session with synthetic identifiers only.
- Record actual steps, observed results, date, environment, and failures in a concise test log.
- **Completion test:** another person can repeat the demo and understand exactly what it proves and does not prove.

## Stage 3 — Evaluation design
- If WCFB is interested, ask staff to choose one workflow.
- Agree in writing on purpose, participants, duration, boundaries, success measures, and stop conditions.
- Use synthetic data only; no system access, installation, live integrations, outbound messages, or procedure changes.
- **Completion test:** WCFB understands and accepts the scope and can freely decline.

## Stage 4 — Business readiness
- Verify Washington State and Seattle registration/licensing requirements for the exact activity with an appropriate adviser or agency.
- Check insurance requirements before onsite activity or any later operational pilot.
- Prepare privacy, security, data handling/deletion, incident response, support, and responsibility documentation before any real data or system access.
- **Completion test:** legal, insurance, privacy, security, operational, and acceptance questions are resolved for the proposed scope.

## Near-term sequence

| When | Milestone | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Now | Leadership package review | Proposal, eight-slide outline/script, scenarios, and current-operations/value brief available for founder review. |
| Next work session | Technical rehearsal | One repeatable fictional-data demo and short test log. |
| After rehearsal | Evaluation brief | One-page scope, success measures, data boundaries, and stop conditions. |
| Before any operational pilot | Readiness gate | Written authorization and legal, insurance, privacy, security, operational, and acceptance review. |

## Explicitly not complete
- No WCFB system integration.
- No live operational pilot.
- No production authorization enforcement or permanent audit service.
- No verified operational performance improvement.
- No verified WCFB internal process audit.
