## What this project is

A limited exploratory evaluation of whether a human-centered accountability pattern could make one staff-selected food-bank workflow easier to understand. The pattern is:

**REQUEST → AUTHORITY → PERMISSION → EVIDENCE → HUMAN REVIEW → OUTCOME → CORRECTION**

The intent is to complement—not replace—existing staff judgment, policy, systems, and partner processes.

## Important boundaries

- All examples are fictional; use synthetic data only.
- No live connection to White Center Food Bank systems or CERVIS.
- No live dispatch, route optimization, driver tracking, delivery verification, food-safety monitoring or approval.
- No production-grade audit-retention or compliance-certification claim.
- No assumptions that WCFB has deficiencies; staff define whether a problem exists.
- No external email or presentation is authorized by these review documents alone.

Private Bruce-to-Bruce handoff notes, personal details, account identifiers, and infrastructure secrets are intentionally excluded.
